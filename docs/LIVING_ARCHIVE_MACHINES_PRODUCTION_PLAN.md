# Living Archive Machines Production Plan

Working tagline:

Born as machines. Aged by time. Marked by transfers. Crowned by sales. Alive with the chain.

## 1. Core Direction

Living Archive Machines is a 3333-piece PFP NFT collection built from editable Mechanical Canvas parts.

Each NFT is born with fixed visual identity traits. The holder does not freely swap identity traits. Instead, the NFT lives through animation, local assembly, chain state, time, transfer history, and milestone states.

The owner experience:

1. The NFT appears dismantled in the animation page.
2. The owner drags parts into place.
3. A local magnet assist helps parts snap into target sockets.
4. Assembly progress is saved only in the browser.
5. If the NFT transfers, the new holder rebuilds it again locally.
6. No wallet signature is required for assembly.
7. On-chain data later drives live animation, but local building is browser-only.

The final art direction:

- Clean collectible PFP composition.
- Slight 3/4 character angle.
- Large readable machine head.
- Shoulders and upper arms visible.
- Teal, dark archive, or related machine backgrounds.
- Dark mechanical body, brass/gold trim, cyan/green glow.
- Semi-cartoon illustrated machine style.
- Detailed but readable at thumbnail size.
- Built from editable Mechanical Canvas pieces, not single pasted character PNGs.

## 2. Golden Rules

No trait is approved unless it has:

1. Visual identity.
2. Idle animation.
3. Chain reaction.
4. Aging or history reaction.
5. At least one connection point.
6. Trait quality score of 3 or higher.

Do not approve traits that are only static decorations.

Every trait should feel like part of a living machine system.

## 3. Trait Quality Score

Score every trait before adding it to the main collection.

- 0 = static, reject
- 1 = tiny animation only, weak
- 2 = clear movement but no chain meaning
- 3 = reacts to chain state
- 4 = reacts to chain, time, and transfer history
- 5 = connects with other traits and has rare states

Main collection traits must score 3+.

Score 5 traits become legendary or mythic candidates.

## 4. Required Trait Questions

For every planned trait, answer:

- What is the trait's idle animation?
- What reacts to every new block?
- What breaks, glitches, shakes, boils, or stresses when gas is high?
- What becomes visually better when holder bond is strong?
- What permanent mark does transfer history leave?
- What rare state unlocks after major milestones?
- Can this trait connect to other traits?
- Can this trait affect another part?
- What connection points does it expose?
- What does it emit?
- What does it receive?
- What traits is it compatible with?
- What traits is it incompatible with?
- What is its animation cost?

## 5. Reaction Categories

Every trait should include at least one of these reaction categories:

- Heartbeat reaction: reacts to new blocks.
- Pressure reaction: reacts to gas/base fee pressure.
- Age reaction: reacts to time since mint.
- Bond reaction: reacts to current holder holding time.
- History reaction: reacts to transfers.
- Milestone reaction: reacts to verified sale tiers or rare events.
- Connection reaction: reacts to nearby or linked traits.
- Mood reaction: calm, stressed, overheated, ancient, legendary.

## 6. Connection System

The strongest traits should not animate alone. They should communicate.

Examples:

- CRT TV Head + Antenna Backpack: antenna signal waves affect screen glitch.
- Clock Head + Gear Collar: collar gears drive clock hands.
- Diving Helmet + Fluid Tube Jacket: bubbles move from jacket tube into helmet.
- Radio Head + Speaker Chest: sound wave moves across both traits.
- Liquid Tank Head + Pressure Hose: gas pressure makes liquid boil through the hose.
- Slot Machine Head + Gold Chain: jackpot pulse lights the chain.
- Typewriter Head + Archive Tag: typed paper adds transfer stamps.

## 7. Global Life Motion

Every assembled NFT should feel alive even before special chain events.

Global animation layer:

- Whole character subtly bounces by 1-4 pixels.
- Parts have tiny independent mechanical vibration.
- Head has slight lag behind body.
- Tubes and liquid pulse softly.
- Gears have micro idle rotation.
- Glass/core glow breathes.
- High gas increases shake and stress.
- Strong holder bond makes movement smoother.
- Old NFTs move slower and heavier.

This is global behavior, not a trait.

## 12. Rarity Strategy

Use weighted rarity, not equal random chaos.

Suggested rarity bands:

- Common: 55%
- Uncommon: 25%
- Rare: 12%
- Epic: 6%
- Legendary: 1.5%
- Mythic: 0.5%

Rules:

- No exact duplicate trait hash.
- Head and hat compatibility must be enforced.
- Expression must match head family.
- Connection traits should be paired often enough to be visible.
- Legendary traits should have stronger connection reactions.
- Rare states should not break thumbnail readability.

## 14. Production Phases

### Phase 1: Trait Bible

Build the production trait catalog with approval rules.

Deliverables:

- Reaction catalog schema.
- First 30-50 approved trait definitions.
- Head animation plan.
- Clothes animation plan.
- Connection rules.

Exit condition:

- At least 10 heads, 10 clothes, 10 accessories, and 10 connection reactions approved.

### Phase 2: Perfect Base Body

Create one strong base body pose.

Deliverables:

- Base body assembly layout.
- Raw machine body.
- Transparent mechanical body guides.
- Target sockets for local assembly.

Exit condition:

- Body looks good with no clothes and with at least 3 clothes.

### Phase 3: Production Head Set 1

Build the first 10 high-quality heads.

Deliverables:

- 10 editable head assemblies.
- Idle animation per head.
- Pressure reaction per head.
- Connection points per head.

Exit condition:

- Every head scores 3+.
- At least 3 heads score 5.

### Phase 4: Clothes Set 1

Build transparent/mechanical clothes.

Deliverables:

- 8-12 clothes assemblies.
- Glass clothes variants.
- Fluid tube jacket.
- Gear collar or neck-driven outfit.

Exit condition:

- Clothes preserve visible mechanics underneath.

### Phase 5: Connection Prototype

Prove linked trait behavior.

Prototype combinations:

- TV + antenna.
- Clock + gear collar.
- Helmet + fluid jacket.
- Radio + speaker chest.

Exit condition:

- Connected traits visibly affect each other in animation.

## 16. Current Assumptions

- Fixed identity traits are assigned at generation/reveal.
- The holder cannot freely swap official identity traits.
- The assembly game is local browser-only.
- Transfer resets local assembly for the new holder because local storage belongs to the browser, not the NFT.
- On-chain history animation comes later.
- OpenSea `animation_url` is the main live experience.
- Static `image` remains a clean PFP render.
- Editable Mechanical Canvas layouts remain available for production and animation.

## 17. North Star

This project should feel like:

An archive of machine-headed characters that are born fixed, rebuilt by their holders, and slowly become more alive and historic as the chain writes time, pressure, transfers, and milestones onto them.
