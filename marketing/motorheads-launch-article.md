# MotorHeads: Living Archive Machines on Ethereum

MotorHeads is a 5,555-piece collection of interactive machine-headed NFTs on Ethereum. Each piece is built from mechanical canvas parts: heads, chassis, bags, clothes, counters, scars, gears, plates, glowing cores, and machine signals that feel assembled rather than stamped out.

The first thing you see is the image. The second thing you discover is that each MotorHead has an interactive animation: dismantle the machine, drag its parts back into place, assemble it, stop the motion, and watch the machine become alive again.

This is not a burn collection. It is not a reroll collection. The base identity of every MotorHead is fixed at mint.

What changes over time is the machine's story.

## Built From Parts

MotorHeads started as a mechanical builder system. Every machine is composed from layered parts that can be moved, tested, dismantled, assembled, and rendered. That matters because the collection is designed for a future owner editor, not just a static reveal.

The base traits include:

- Head
- Chassis
- Clothes
- Back accessory
- Neck accessory
- Core
- Fluid/material behavior
- Background
- Sale counter style
- Block counter style
- Visual edition

The collection also includes rare full-gold editions. There are 55 full-gold MotorHeads in total. The first 10 are treasury mints, and the remaining 45 are hidden lucky positions inside the 5,555 supply.

## Interactive From Mint

Each NFT includes an animation page. On OpenSea, collectors can open the animation and use three simple controls:

- Dismantle
- Assemble
- Stop/start motion

The drag-and-assemble system is intentionally playful. The machine begins as scattered parts, then becomes a complete MotorHead when assembled.

## The Alive Layer

MotorHeads is being built with an “alive” roadmap: a future website where owners can connect their wallet, awaken their machine, customize visual overlays, add extra parts, and let the MotorHead evolve from wallet history.

The current mint already includes metadata hooks for:

- Owner customization
- Locked base DNA
- Editable visual slots
- Transfer scars
- Sale counters
- Holder-bond evolution
- Future agent profile support
- Future registry/backend state

The important principle is simple: the base NFT is permanent, but the life layer can grow on top of it.

## Why MotorHeads Exists

Most NFT collections stop at the image. MotorHeads starts with the image, then adds a machine behind it: interactive assembly, locked traits, future customization, and an evolving archive layer.

It is a collection for people who like strange machines, mechanical art, on-chain objects, and the feeling that a digital collectible can become more personal the longer it stays with its owner.
